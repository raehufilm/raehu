# Miu Miu 2022 CNY

On the eve of the new year, when the doors of a cinematic story are opened, the paths of two different characters whose lives go in different directions intersect in the spiritual Miu Miu universe.

In Lexie Liu and Qiu Tian's cinematic mind rendezvous, we get a familiar feeling of déjà-vu. One reads her own lines, the other hums a familiar tune. This is a timeless relationship established through sound and space. It's both a curious sweetness and a free fall into memories. There is a dialogue between them that salutes abstract nature. This exquisite synergy between the two brings their shining glances together on a common denominator – their punctual friendship, that when established just in time, opens a whole new curtain before them.
