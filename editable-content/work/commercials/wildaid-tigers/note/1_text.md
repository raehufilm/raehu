# WildAid Tigers

Because of Our Efforts, Tigers are Making a Comeback.

Chinese martial artist, actor, film director and WildAid ambassador Wu Jing calls for protecting Siberian tigers and their habitats in WildAid’s newest campaign in partnership with the Northeast China Tiger and Leopard National Park (NCTLNP) and China Wildlife Conservation Association.

The campaign launched today across 20 cities in China to showcase the achievements made by the NCTLNP in restoring and protecting the ecosystem, cracking down on wildlife crime, and protecting flagship species such as the Siberian tiger.
