# Handsome Factory Barbershop Hong Kong

JBS is a Hong Kong actor and a member of the Hong Kong hip-hop group 24 Herbs.

Born in Indonesia, JBS moved to Hong Kong at the age of 11. He later went to Tokyo, Japan, to study Japanese, and is fluent in English, Cantonese, Mandarin, Japanese, and Korean.

In 1999, he founded his own skateboard shop company, "8FIVE2," and became the chairman of the Hong Kong Skateboard Association. Over the years, he has been dedicated to promoting skateboarding and has served as the emcee for various skateboarding events. Skateboarding has become one of the most influential parts of his life.

This film showcases JBS's original intention behind opening his barbershop — to bring community culture into Hong Kong, just as he once popularized skateboarding culture in the city.
