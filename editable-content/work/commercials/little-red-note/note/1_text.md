# outdoor activities in Chinese style 

When I was a kid, heading outdoors with my family meant using a bedsheet as a makeshift tablecloth and munching on leftovers. Sometimes, after a long drive to the mountains, we'd just chill out and do nothing.

Back then, I didn't get the appeal of outdoor activities. But now, I realize it's not about the fancy gear or the cool locations; it's about being together.

I kept the flashback scenes simple, using old photos as references. Oh, and here's a fun fact: I hid a clue in the story! Look out for the red plastic bags in almost every scene. We even changed up the texture to match different eras.

Just like those plastic bags, the way Chinese people express love is all about being real and down-to-earth. It's not about appearances, but about genuine connection and sincerity.
