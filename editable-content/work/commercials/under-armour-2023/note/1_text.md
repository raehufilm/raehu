# UA 2023

Wanna share several challenges i faced while creating this spot:

1. The film needed to capture the essence of athletes conquering nature and enduring harsh weather before sunrise. However, we had only two days to shoot, and to add to the challenge, it was during the hottest days in Chongqing in August, which was off-season for our theme. As a solution, I designed numerous indoor scenes showing the characters preparing for their journey. This move allowed us to use daylight for night scenes, reserving the precious magic hour for the outdoor shots.

2. The product we were showcasing was down jackets, not typically meant for active use. Nevertheless, we had to maintain the brand’s athletic identity. This meant the film had to culminate with the characters engaging in some sports activities. As a result, the film maintained a pattern of ”calm before action“ throughout, ensuring that the dynamic sports footage didn‘t overpower the main storyline. Additionally, to create the right visual atmosphere, we minimized blocking and emphasized static camera movements. Our extensive location scouting paid off, and I think DP did a great job.

3. We put a lot of effort into ensuring the authenticity of the rain and snow CG effects. In the early stages, we made several preparations to make them appear as realistic as possible. For instance, we designed specific scenes for rain interaction with glass surfaces, like car windows, and deliberately created a winter fog effect using a bathroom mirror.
