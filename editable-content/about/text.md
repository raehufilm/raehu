# about

Rae Hu 樂瑞 is a director and filmmaker based in Shanghai, working with creative partners across China, the US, Europe, and Latin America.

She has spent the past decade making commercial work for global brands, while developing an independent voice in parallel.

Her short film *Strange Fruit* has screened internationally and won multiple awards. Her debut feature, currently in development, follows a grown woman learning to be honest again, set in Mexico City.

She works in English, conversational Spanish, Mandarin, and Cantonese.
