# about

Rae Hu 樂瑞 is a director and artist based in Shanghai, working at the intersection of independent cinema and commercial film, with creative partners across China, the US, Europe, and Latin America.

She produced We Don't Dance for Nothing, a feature about the Filipina domestic workers of Hong Kong finding freedom in their weekly street dances — screened internationally and the recipient of multiple awards. Her own short film, Strange Fruits — the story of a six-year-old girl learning to lie for the first time — has also reached festivals abroad. Her debut feature, currently in development, follows a grown woman learning to be honest again: the same question, asked at the other end of a life, set in Mexico City.

Across more than a decade, she has directed commercial work for global brands including Google, Samsung, Gucci, Adidas, and Lego — bringing the same instinct for finding the true thing inside a brief to campaigns built at scale.

She works across Mandarin, Cantonese, English, and conversational Spanish.
